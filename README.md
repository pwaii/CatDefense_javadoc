## 🎮 Game Overview
Cat Defense challenges players to defend a central base against autonomous waves of enemies. Success depends on your ability to manage your currency, choose the right towers for the job, and clear the environment to make room for your feline-inspired fortifications.

### Core Features
*   **Dual Difficulty:** Choose between **Easy Mode** and **Hard Mode** depending on your skill level.
*   **Dynamic Environments:** Interactive obstacles (Flowers) block your path—clear them to gain extra gold and building space.
*   **Strategic Upgrades:** Invest currency back into your towers to scale their damage and range as waves get tougher.
*   **Custom Audio:** Integrated settings to toggle background music and sound effects.

## ⚔️ Gameplay Mechanics

### 🏗️ Defensive Towers
Towers can only be placed on unoccupied **Grass tiles**. They cannot be placed on the road.

| Tower Type | DMG | Range | Price | CD | Max Lv | Special Ability |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Archer** | 10 | 150 | 25 | 1.00 | 3 | Consistent single-target DPS. |
| **Laser** | 5 | 250 | 50 | 0.55 | 5 | High fire rate and long reach. |
| **Fire** | 10 | 150 | 30 | 2.00 | 4 | **Splash:** AOE damage in 80-unit radius. |
| **Ice** | 2 | 140 | 50 | 1.50 | 3 | **Slow:** Reduces enemy speed by 50%. |

> [!TIP]
> **Upgrade Path:** Upgrading a tower significantly boosts stats. For example, the Archer Tower gains +5 Damage and +20 Range per level!

### 👾 Enemy Bestiary
Enemies move along a fixed path. If they reach your base, they deal damage equal to their **remaining health**.

| Enemy Type | HP | Speed | Reward |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Normal / Witch** | 25 / 50 | 2 / 3 | 10 / 20 |
| **Fast / Fast Witch** | 10 / 30 | 5 / 6 | 15 / 20 |
| **Boss / Boss Witch** | 100 | 2 / 4 | 50 / 40 |

### 🌸 Environment (Obstacles)
Don't let the flowers fool you—they are taking up prime real estate! Click them repeatedly to remove them.

*   **Element I:** 30 HP | 10 Gold Reward
*   **Element II:** 60 HP | 15 Gold Reward
*   **Element III:** 120 HP | 20 Gold Reward

## 🕹️ How to Play

1.  **Launch:** Select your difficulty from the Main Menu.
2.  **Build:** Click on a Grass tile and select a tower from your arsenal.
3.  **Clear:** Click on Flowers to clear tiles and earn quick cash.
4.  **Manage:** Use the **Pause Menu** (top-right) if you need to take a break or adjust audio.
5.  **Win:** Survive the final wave with at least 1 Base HP remaining.
